Ninja Gaiden II for PS5 - texture packs (optional).

A texture pack is made on a PC with the Ninja Gaiden II PC recompilation's
texture tools (dump, upscale, pack). Copy the pack's folder here:

  /data/homebrew/PPSA94202/Texture Packs/<pack name>/pack/...

then choose it in the hub: SETTINGS > Textures. Larger packs need more
memory; 2x packs are the recommended size on PS5.
